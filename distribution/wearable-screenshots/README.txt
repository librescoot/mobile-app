Librescoot Wear OS Play listing screenshots

Three opaque RGB PNGs captured directly from the app running on a Wear OS 5.1 emulator (454x454). They meet Play Console’s 1:1, 384–3,840 px and 8 MB limits.

The on-screen “Demo scooter” battery values, state and range are synthetic sample data seeded only in the emulator; they are not measurements from a real scooter. No scooter was connected and no vehicle action was sent.
