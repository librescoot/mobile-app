## Librescoot App for unu 1.4.4-beta.1

- Pair a connected scooter with a supported online account through Librescoot Connect, without using the scooter display.
- Review the scooter and data-sharing notice before starting, then choose and confirm the account on the Connect website.
- The messages screen and SMS forwarding settings are translated in all supported languages.

Connect pairing requires compatible scooter firmware and an internet connection on the scooter. Test with the scooter safely parked.
